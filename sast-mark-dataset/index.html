How to label
  • Label what is ACTUALLY on the paper, not what was intended.
  • If a row has two marks, pick the main one, choose "other", and write a note.
  • An erased or crossed-out mark counts as "other".
  • Photograph the whole form flat, in good light, with no fingers covering it.
